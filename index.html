Key findings

5 findings · select one to read it

The MLC work names Francisco Vargas as a writer, so the registry already knows he co-wrote it. But no publisher or administrator is attached to his seat, and the writer record carries no IPI number. The MLC pays publishers, not writers, so a writer seat with nothing attached to it is a share that accrues to the unclaimed pool. Ñengo Flow's side, by contrast, is fully wired: Real G 4 Life Inc as original publisher, Cinq Songs as administrator, 50% on file.

A search on ISRC UST8K1768870 returns no work at all, and the work itself shows zero matched recordings. The MLC pays against recordings that are matched to a work; an unmatched recording earns for nobody, including the publisher that has already claimed. This is a linkage problem rather than a registration problem, and it is the cheaper of the two to fix.

The MLC writer record that sits on this work (nine works in total) also carries a Mexican regional writer's compositions from the EMI catalog and an unrelated Spanish rap credit. That is what happens when a writer is registered by name with no IPI. Any claim on Chocolate Blanco's behalf should attach to a writer identity with an IPI, so his share cannot be confused with a namesake's.

Two other Chocolate Blanco compositions are on file at The MLC with claims attached: "Mejor Que Ayer" (2016) carries a 25% CD Baby claim, and "A Ella Le Gusta Rough" carries 50% through Champion Sound Publisher, administered by Mayimba Music. Both are registered under the stage name with no IPI. The CD Baby rail in particular could be used to claim Ella Me Llama today without any new registration.

Global Music Rights has no record, which is expected for a song of this profile. BMI and ASCAP have not yet been checked for this title; that lookup will show the performance split, Ñengo Flow's society, and whether Chocolate Blanco holds a U.S. society seat at all. His home society, if any, is more likely SCD in Chile, which only the writer can confirm.

Recommended next steps

  1. Confirm the writer identity and get an IPI on the seat

    Establish whether Chocolate Blanco already holds an IPI through SCD or any other society. If he does, the claim attaches to that number. If he does not, the fastest route is a U.S. society application under the Writer & Publisher option, after confirming he is not already affiliated abroad.

  2. Claim the open 50% at The MLC

    Attach a publisher or administrator to Francisco Vargas's writer seat on work EC7XZB. Because the work already exists, this is a claim on an existing record rather than a new registration, and it requires evidence of the split — a split sheet, the original agreement with Ñengo Flow's camp, or written confirmation from Real G 4 Life / Cinq Songs.

  3. Link the recording

    Submit ISRC UST8K1768870 against EC7XZB so the recording matches the work. This unlocks payment on both halves, and it is the step with the fastest effect on money already accruing.

  4. Pull the BMI/ASCAP record

    One Songview lookup completes the picture on the performance side and tells us whether a society registration also needs to be made.

  5. Fix the label metadata going forward

    The recording carries "Chocolate Blanco" as its only songwriter string. Legal names and IPIs in distributor metadata are what allow registries to match automatically; correcting this prevents the same gap on re-releases and future work.

Basis and limits. This is a draft analysis prepared from public registry and DSP data for a single composition. It establishes what is registered, what is claimed, and what is attributable. It does not and cannot establish actual payment or royalty flow, which is visible only inside a society or MLC account.
