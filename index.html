Key findings

5 findings · select one to read it

The MLC work names Francisco Vargas as a writer, so the registry already knows he co-wrote it. But no publisher or administrator is attached to his seat, and the writer record carries no IPI number. The MLC pays publishers, not writers, so a writer seat with nothing attached to it is a share that accrues to the unclaimed pool. Ñengo Flow's side, by contrast, is fully wired: Real G 4 Life Inc as original publisher, Cinq Songs as administrator, 50% on file.

A search on ISRC UST8K1768870 returns no work at all, and the work itself shows zero matched recordings. The MLC pays against recordings that are matched to a work; an unmatched recording earns for nobody, including the publisher that has already claimed. This is a linkage problem rather than a registration problem, and it is the cheaper of the two to fix.

The MLC writer record that sits on this work (nine works in total) also carries a Mexican regional writer's compositions from the EMI catalog and an unrelated Spanish rap credit. That is what happens when a writer is registered by name with no IPI. Any claim on Chocolate Blanco's behalf should attach to a writer identity with an IPI, so his share cannot be confused with a namesake's.

Two other Chocolate Blanco compositions are on file at The MLC with claims attached: "Mejor Que Ayer" (2016) carries a 25% CD Baby claim, and "A Ella Le Gusta Rough" carries 50% through Champion Sound Publisher, administered by Mayimba Music. Both are registered under the stage name with no IPI. The CD Baby rail in particular could be used to claim Ella Me Llama today without any new registration.

BMI Songview, which shows both BMI and ASCAP repertoire, has no registration of this song: the 80 works with this title belong to other writers, and pairing the title with Ñengo Flow's writer identity (VAZQUEZ EDWIN ROSA, IPI 489835584) or with either performer returns nothing. Chocolate Blanco is visible there on two other works, in both cases as a non-society writer with no IPI, so performance royalties on anything he writes currently have no one to be paid to. Global Music Rights has no record either, which is expected for a song of this profile. His home society, if any, is most likely SCD in Chile, which only the writer can confirm.

What happens next

  1. Confirm your society — and join one if you have not

    This is the first step, because it governs everything else. We searched BMI and ASCAP and found no registration of this song and no writer identity with an IPI number for you; the only trace is a non-society writer entry with a zero IPI on two other works. So either you are affiliated with a society outside the United States, most likely SCD in Chile, or you are not affiliated anywhere yet. Tell us which, and if you already have a society, send us your IPI number — it is the number that identifies you as a writer everywhere in the world.

    If you are not affiliated yet, ASCAP is the cheaper way in. As a new member, joining as Writer & Publisher together is free. Applying as a writer alone is also free, but adding the publisher side later costs $50 — so do both in the same application. This matters more than the $50: performance royalties are split into a writer share and a publisher share, and without a publisher account the publisher half of your own songs has nowhere to go. BMI charges a one-time application fee for writers and a larger one for publishers, shown at the final step of their application; check their current amounts before deciding.

    ASCAP — Writer & Publisher · free BMI — songwriter application BMI — publisher application

    On the ASCAP form choose New Member, then the Writer & Publisher option — that is the one that is free. Selecting Writer alone is also free, but you would then pay $50 to add the publisher side later.

    Two things to know before you apply. You can only belong to one U.S. performing rights organisation at a time — if you are already with a society, switching requires a release letter from your current one before you can join another. And if you are already affiliated abroad, joining a U.S. society is a real decision rather than a formality; talk to us first so we do not create a second identity that competes with the one you already have.

  2. Establish your writer identity on this song

    The MLC work already carries a writer seat that reads Francisco Vargas, with no IPI attached and no publisher behind it. With your IPI in hand, that seat becomes yours: we attach the number so your share can no longer be confused with the other writers who share that name in the registry. What we need from you is whatever exists in writing for the split with Ñengo Flow's side — a split sheet, the original agreement, or written confirmation from Real G 4 Life or Cinq Songs, who administer his half. Anything in writing helps, and nothing is filed without it.

  3. Claim the open 50% at The MLC

    Because the composition already exists as work EC7XZB, this is a claim on an existing record rather than a new registration. A publisher or administrator is attached to your writer seat, and the 50% that is currently unclaimed moves to being on file in your name.

  4. Link the recording

    Submit ISRC UST8K1768870 against EC7XZB so the recording matches the work. This is the step with the fastest effect on money already accruing, because it unlocks payment on both halves — including the half that is already claimed and is still not being paid on this recording.

  5. Register the composition with your society

    Nothing exists for this song at BMI or ASCAP. Once your identity is settled, the composition is registered with your society so that performance royalties — from radio, streaming and live use across the countries in your audience — have somewhere to go. Ñengo Flow's side can be registered by his own publisher against the same work.

  6. Fix the metadata going forward

    The recording carries "Chocolate Blanco" as its only songwriter string. Legal names and IPI numbers in distributor metadata are what allow registries to match automatically; correcting this costs nothing and prevents the same gap on re-releases and future work.
